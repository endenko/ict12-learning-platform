# ICT12 Platform design
Standalone Vietnamese ICT12 practice and assessment platform; vanilla responsive frontend, Vercel Node API, isolated Supabase private JSON document store with deny-all RLS and secret-gated RPC. Server-only bank and scoring. Issued accounts, admin controls, history and diagnostic analytics. No public registration. Browser controls are deterrence, not screenshot prevention.
Book verified: topic 1=A AI (lessons 1–2), topic 2=B networks (3–5), topic 3=C digital ethics (6), NOT storage/search. Keep A-only deployment unchanged.
